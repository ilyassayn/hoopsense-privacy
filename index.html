Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HoopSense ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application on Android or iOS. HoopSense provides basketball game analysis and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA. By using HoopSense, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Device Identifier
We store an identifier generated for your installation of the app. It is used to manage your subscription status, unlocked content, prediction credits, favourite teams, and notification preferences. If you sign in, this identifier is linked to your account email address so that your purchases can be recognised on another device.

Sign in with Google or Apple (optional)
Signing in is optional; you can use the app without it. If you sign in, the app receives the email address of your Google Account or Apple ID and sends it to our servers, where it is stored only to link your purchases across devices. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive only the relay address Apple provides. We never receive your password.

Purchase Information
When you make an in-app purchase, Google Play or the Apple App Store provides us with a purchase token or transaction identifier and a product ID, which we store to verify and activate your subscription or credits and to handle refunds. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Notification Token
If you allow push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) generates a device token. We use it to deliver prediction alerts and game results, and occasionally to inform you about new features, subscription offers, or promotions. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Language, Country and Time Zone
We store your app language and an approximate country (derived from your network address when you register, not from GPS) so that content, regional pricing and notification times match your region. We do not collect your precise location.

Support Messages
If you contact us through the in-app support form, we receive the contact address, subject and message you enter. These are stored on our servers and forwarded to a private internal channel so that we can answer you.

Usage Analytics
We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. It records events such as screen views, content unlocks and purchase events, together with an app instance identifier. You can turn usage analytics off in the app (VIP tab → Account & Data). This data is not sold and is not shared with advertising networks — the app shows no advertising, contains no third-party advertising SDKs, and does not use your device's Advertising ID.

These providers act as processors on our behalf. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver daily basketball game analysis and predictions
  • To manage your subscription, credits, favourites, and unlocked content
  • To verify, restore, and refund in-app purchases
  • To send prediction and result notifications (only with your permission)
  • To occasionally inform you about new features, subscription offers, or promotions via notifications (only with your permission)
  • To answer your support requests
  • To measure how the app is used so we can improve performance and user experience

4. Data Retention

Your device identifier and the data associated with it (email address if you signed in, subscription status, credits, purchase records, notification preferences) are stored on our servers for as long as you use the app. We keep purchase records for as long as required to honour subscriptions, process refunds, and meet accounting and legal obligations.

You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time, from inside the app (VIP tab → Account & Data → Delete my account and data); this takes effect immediately. You can also ask us to do it — see Section 8. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Our servers and database are hosted in the EU and access is restricted to authorized personnel only. We do not store payment card information at any point.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. Data is shared only with the service providers listed in Section 2, to the extent necessary to operate the app, or where required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

HoopSense is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time.

In the app: open the VIP tab, scroll to Account & Data and tap Delete my account and data. Your account record and everything linked to it, including unlock history and support messages, are deleted immediately. Deleting your data does not cancel a paid subscription — cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

By request: use the form at ilyassayn.github.io/data-deletion, or contact us at the email address below and include the email address you signed in with, if any. We complete such requests within 30 days.

Two things are deliberately not erased by deletion. Purchase records are kept for as long as required to honour an active subscription, process refunds and meet accounting and legal obligations. And an irreversible one-way value derived from your device identifier is kept: it cannot be reversed, it identifies nobody, and its only purpose is to stop the same device from claiming a one-time welcome credit or single-use promotion code again after deleting and reinstalling.

You can also stop future data collection by disabling notifications and analytics in the app, and by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notification. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
